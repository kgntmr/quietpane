Quietpane - HOW TO USE
Developed by KomodoWorks.com  |  free  |  open source  |  collects nothing


1. UNZIP IT
   Quietpane cannot start from inside the zip file.
   Right-click Quietpane.zip  >  Extract All  >  Extract.

2. START IT
   In the new folder, double-click "Start Quietpane".
   You never need to open the "App files" folder.

   Quietpane isn't digitally signed yet, so Windows asks a few questions:
   - "Windows protected your PC"          -> "More info", then "Run anyway"
   - "Do you want to run this file?"      -> "Run"
   - "Allow this app to make changes?"    -> "Yes"

3. CLICK ONE BUTTON
   Click "Quiet my PC now", then "Yes". It shows what it will do first and
   takes about a minute. Restart your PC when it says "All done!".
   Your drivers are never touched.


ALSO IN THE APP
   Health     Opens with one line telling you how your PC is doing, so you
              aren't left to work it out from a screen of numbers. Under it,
              matching tiles for the processor, graphics, memory and drive -
              each with its figure, how warm it is in plain words, and the
              last two minutes drawn underneath. Then one list of whatever
              is keeping your PC busiest, and how the battery and drive are
              holding up. The drive's heat and wear come from the drive
              itself, because Windows' own figures for it are often invented.
              "Watch this session" keeps an eye on it while you work and
              tells you afterwards how hot it got, when it was held back
              to cool off, and how far memory went. It speaks up once if
              something is worth interrupting for. It only watches while
              Quietpane is open, and writes nothing down.
              "How it has been holding up" is Windows' own score out of
              ten, with what has crashed in the last month. A session can
              be saved to your Desktop as one page to keep or send on.
   Privacy    Your browser add-ons, and what each one is allowed
              to read - "reads and changes everything on every site you
              visit" - with a tick box to switch off the ones you don't
              want. Also which apps used your camera, microphone and
              location, and when, and what is talking to the internet
              right now.
   Apps       Choose what starts when you sign in - and see what each one
              is costing you, heaviest first.
   Free up    "Worth clearing first" finds the easy room: installers you
    space     have already used, downloads from years ago, big files you
              have not changed in ages. One button sends them to the
              Recycle Bin. Under it, "Where your space went" shows the
              biggest folders and files, so you can see where it all went.
   About      One button puts Quietpane in your Start menu and on your
              desktop, and a tick box starts it when you sign in. Both
              keep working even if you move or delete this folder.
              Another tick box has it tell you - with a small badge on
              its taskbar icon - if Windows switches things back on.
              Quietpane follows Windows' light or dark setting; under
              Appearance you can fix it to Light or Dark instead.
   Undo       Changed your mind? Everything recorded can be put back.
              Cleaned-up files are in your Recycle Bin.

   Open Quietpane again now and then. Big Windows updates sometimes switch
   things back on, and Home will tell you what came back.

   Every line is kept short. Point at anything - or Tab to it - to see the
   full explanation.

   Quietpane works with just a keyboard (Tab, arrow keys, Enter, Esc, and
   F5 to look again) and with screen readers such as Narrator.

JUST WANT A CHECK-UP THAT CHANGES NOTHING?
   Double-click "Safety scan only". For anything it finds, you choose what
   happens next - nothing is removed behind your back.

A GOOD START, NOT A GUARANTEE
   Quietpane clears out the usual troublemakers, but it cannot promise a
   PC is clean. If yours still feels wrong, run a deeper scan with a
   dedicated security tool too.

A WINDOW FULL OF CODE OPENED?
   You opened one of the app's own files. Close it (pick nothing if
   Windows asks what to open it with) and double-click "Start Quietpane".

REMOVING QUIETPANE
   Removing it does not undo its changes, so use Undo first if you want
   your PC back exactly as it was. If you added shortcuts or the sign-in
   start, take them away in About - Quietpane's copy in Program Files
   goes to the Recycle Bin with them. Then delete the Quietpane folder
   and any Quietpane-Report files on your Desktop.
   Your undo history stays in C:\ProgramData\Quietpane - delete that too
   once you're sure you won't need it.
